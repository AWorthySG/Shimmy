## Reviews

1. In the profile, choose **Ask for reviews** and copy the short link.
2. Add it in Vercel as `GOOGLE_REVIEW_URL`. The site then emails clients who left an email address 14 days after their appointment.
3. Reply to every review within a few days, including the critical ones.
